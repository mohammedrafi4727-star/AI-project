Academic Curriculum & Evaluation System

LPU Guidelines

Course credit structures, MTE & ETE pattern breakdown, and Continuous Assessment scoring.

Continuous Assessment (CA)

25 - 30% Marks
  • Format: 4 evaluations conducted per course during semester.
  • Calculation Policy: Best 3 out of 4 scores are selected automatically.
  • Component Types: MCQ test on LMS, coding task, term paper, group presentation, or lab performance.
  • Missed CA: No re-tests are typically scheduled except under genuine verified medical leave approved by HOS.

Mid-Term Examination (MTE)

20 - 25% Marks
  • Timing: Mid-semester (approx 7-8 weeks from term start).
  • Syllabus: Units 1 to 3 (50% coverage).
  • Total Marks: 50 Marks (scaled down proportionally in UMS).
  • Pattern: Section A (Objective/Short answer) + Section B (Analytical / Descriptive 5 & 10 markers).
  • Seating: Seating plan is published in UMS 2 days prior.

End-Term Examination (ETE)

45 - 50% Marks
  • Timing: End of semester (May for Spring, Dec for Autumn).
  • Syllabus: Full syllabus (Units 1 to 6).
  • Total Marks: 100 Marks (3 Hours duration).
  • Prerequisite: Minimum 75% attendance mandatory to generate Admit Card in UMS.
  • Backlog / Re-appear: If overall score <40% (or 'E'/'F' grade), registration in summer term or re-appear required.

UMS & RMS Ticket Resolution Guide

Never get confused by the hundreds of links inside University Management System (UMS). Follow this verified roadmap to lodge an RMS request or solve fee and attendance disputes.

Step-by-Step: How to Lodge an RMS Ticket

Official procedure to get issues resolved within 24-48 hours

Open UMS Portal
1

Log in to UMS

Visit ums.lpu.in with your Registration ID and Password. Complete 2-factor OTP if prompted.

Nav: Home Dashboard
2

Locate RMS Menu

Open the Left Navigation Menu > Click Relationship Management System (RMS) > Select Lodge Request.

Nav: RMS > Lodge Request
3

Select Right Category

Pick appropriate category (e.g., Examination, Account & Fees, Hostel Services, or Academics) to route directly to your HOD/Officer.

Filter: Department Specific
4

Attach Proof & Submit

Clearly describe problem, attach medical certificate or payment receipt screenshot if required, then note your Ticket ID.

Turnaround: 24 to 48 Hours

Fee Payment & Late Fine Issues

Fee Deducted but UMS shows Unpaid?

Do not pay twice immediately. Bank gateways take up to 24-48 hours to reconcile. If status hasn't updated after 48h, raise an RMS under Accounts > Fee Reconciliation with the bank UTR number and transaction receipt.

Where to pay offline?

Account cash counters are located in Block 30 & Block 32 Ground Floor. Accepted modes: Demand Draft (DD) or Debit/Credit swipe machine.

Attendance Discrepancy & Medical Leave

Attended class but marked Absent?

Approach the respective course instructor within 48 hours with proof of class participation or notes. Faculty can rectify attendance directly from their portal within 3 days.

Medical Duty Leave submission:

Get medical prescription attested by University Health Center (Shanti Devi Mittal Hospital on campus) first, then upload on UMS under Duty Leave Request within 5 days of returning.
